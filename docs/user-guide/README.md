# HMS user guide

Open **User guide** from an HMS workspace or Help on a supported form, or use `/desk/hms-guide`.

The guide includes source-reviewed instructions and a browser-checked Hospital Live overview updated on 29 October 2026. Full role-by-role workflow and media verification remains pending.

A Hospital Live overview screenshot is available. Other updated screenshots and continuous cursor-visible workflow recordings remain in preparation.

Examples follow Maya Patel, Dr Alex Morgan and Paracetamol 500 mg through the HMS workflows. Contact addresses use example domains; the reminder demonstration does not send email.

Where a GIF is available, use Play walkthrough to start it and Stop walkthrough to return to the still image.

## Getting started

- [Find your way around](start.md) — Find your workspace, records and help.
- [Watch hospital activity in 3D](hospital-live.md) — See action-driven patient characters, doctor rooms, waiting seats and current care activity.

## Reception

- [Find, register and update a patient](registration.md) — Search existing records before creating a patient.
- [Book or reschedule an appointment](booking.md) — Reserve a valid slot with the right doctor and department.
- [Check in a booked or walk-in patient](check-in.md) — Create one consultation visit for the arrival.
- [Manage clinical follow-ups](follow-ups.md) — Work the due queue and link the next appointment.
- [Understand free follow-up eligibility](eligibility.md) — Understand the allowed free visits and inclusive expiry date.

## Administration

- [Review and merge duplicate patients](duplicates.md) — Keep one identity after an explicit duplicate review.
- [Configure staff, doctors and schedules](staff.md) — Link a doctor to a staff account and working hours.
- [Reconcile records after an upgrade](legacy.md) — Establish reviewed opening stock and allocate historical receipts.

## Clinical care

- [Record a consultation and corrections](consultation.md) — Review history, record findings and complete the visit.
- [Request, receive and review a lab report](labs.md) — Keep an uploaded result attached to its own patient and visit.
- [Prescribe and send medication reminders](reminders.md) — Preview a dated schedule and track its email queue.
- [Record a patient leaving before care is finished](departures.md) — Record the actual departure, preserve work already done and assign pending results.

## Inpatient care

- [Admit, allocate a bed and transfer](admission.md) — Create one active admission and keep bed occupancy consistent.
- [Record inpatient care and vitals](care.md) — Add dated care and measurements to the right admission.
- [Finalize discharge or reopen admission](discharge.md) — Finalize the summary and release the bed together.

## Pharmacy

- [Receive medicines and inspect stock lots](intake.md) — Post received batches before they become available stock.
- [Dispense, hand over and return medicines](dispense.md) — Track posted stock, actual handovers and inspected returns separately.
- [Adjust stock with a reason](stock-adjustments.md) — Record a counted difference without erasing movements.

## Billing

- [Issue invoices, record payments and refunds](billing.md) — Reconcile charges, credits, posted receipts and refund dues.

## Reporting

- [Read dashboards and investigate discrepancies](reporting.md) — Use the right view for due work, census, stock and money.
