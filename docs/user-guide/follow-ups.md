# Manage clinical follow-ups

Work the due queue and link the next appointment.

**Who can do this:** Receptionist · Doctor · System Manager

[All workflows](README.md)

## What this workflow does

Clinical follow-ups track the care plan and outreach progress. Fee eligibility tracks whether a consultation can be free; neither one proves the other.

## Before you start

- A saved visit with a Follow up date.

## 1. Set the care plan

On the visit enter Follow up and leave Follow Up Status Pending. Save the record.

## 2. Open the due queue

Open Follow-ups. Due Follow-ups includes Pending or Contacted dates up to today; blank, future and cancelled visits are excluded.

## 3. Record contact progress

After following your hospital’s contact process, update Follow Up Status to Contacted and enter Follow Up Notes. Saving a note does not send a message.

## 4. Book the next appointment

Use Create → Book Follow-up from the source visit. Confirm the date and practitioner, choose a valid slot and save; the source is marked Scheduled.

## 5. Close or restore the queue item

Completion of the linked appointment closes the item. Cancelling the linked appointment restores Pending when no other active booking covers it. Close outreach manually when the care plan is resolved.

## What success looks like

The care plan has a date and a meaningful outreach status; scheduled follow-ups retain their source visit link.

## Common problems and recovery

Do not infer free eligibility from a follow-up date. An empty queue can correctly mean there are no due dated Pending/Contacted visits.
