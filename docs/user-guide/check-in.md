# Check in a booked or walk-in patient

Create one consultation visit for the arrival.

**Who can do this:** Receptionist · System Manager

[All workflows](README.md)

## What this workflow does

Check In converts a saved appointment into a linked Patient Visit. Walk-ins start a visit directly. A clinical episode’s patient, practitioner, department and visit date are retained after creation.

## Before you start

- A saved scheduled appointment, or an active patient and practitioner for a walk-in.

## 1. Confirm arrival details

Open the Scheduled appointment and verify the patient ID, practitioner, date and department. Save any changes first.

## 2. Choose Check In

Select Check In once. Status changes to Checked In and Patient Visit links to the consultation record. Repeating the action reuses that visit.

## 3. Open the linked visit

Follow Patient Visit and confirm Checked In. The doctor can continue the consultation there.

## 4. Register a walk-in instead

From Patient choose Create → New Walk-in Visit. Select Doctor, Department and Visit date, keep Status Checked In and save.

## 5. Review eligibility and charge

The saved visit may link Fee Validity or an Invoice. The clinical follow-up queue is separate from free-fee eligibility.

## What success looks like

One saved visit represents the arrival and links back to its appointment when applicable.

## Common problems and recovery

Do not enter a Patient Visit link manually or reuse a visit for another patient. To cancel an unused episode, resolve its lab requests, any posted medicines and payments first. Fully credited and refunded consultation invoices are retained with their credit history; an unpaid uncredited consultation charge is voided.
